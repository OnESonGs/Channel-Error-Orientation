# Paired controls for the frozen three-coefficient correction

Run `python controls/replay.py --output outputs/controls.json` from the repository root. The script reconstructs 45 frozen-receiver contrasts and their bootstrap quantiles, 56 subgroup summaries, and seven head outputs on four example REs. It compares these results with the recorded values.

## What was frozen

A pilot-based initial estimate is updated on data REs only. Let the two-receiver initial vector be h0, the delay-basis pilot estimate be h_p, and C_e the reference-prior estimation covariance (not an oracle posterior). The new residual adds to h_p − h0 the displacement obtained by applying (I + C_e/t)⁻¹ to h0, normalizing back to the original norm, and subtracting h0. The old residual is h_p − h0 only. For each residual, project onto the radial, common-phase and complex-orthogonal modes of h0; when h0 is zero, assign the residual to the first column. The frozen coefficients multiply these three columns. At inference only this update and the validation-selected common LLR scale are used; the true channel is not an input. See [head.py](head.py) for the forward map; its inputs start **after** pilot estimation. Neither backbone/PHY training nor estimation of C_e from raw pilots is distributed here.

Each estimator/profile has separately chosen coefficients. F pulls the full plug-in curvature back to coefficient space; D diagonalizes in physical coordinates *before* the same pullback. They share the training-step budget, starting point, pilot basis and validation rule. The MSE control uses the new residual but optimizes channel MSE. The rotation-only control follows the fixed covariance displacement without fitting a three-component direction. The scale at the initial estimate was fixed during training backtracking. Validation fits a scale for each candidate and selects nonzero update or identity; coefficients and selected scales are fixed before testing.

## Arrays and units

Each [data/](data/) file named A through D contains numeric arrays (load with `allow_pickle=False`):

- `slot_id[slot,2]`: physical (generation_seed, scenario_id); `snr_db[slot]` is the simulated receive SNR used for bootstrap strata.
- `rates[slot,base,arm]`: mean frozen-scale bit-metric rate, in bits per data RE, over 972 data REs. Positive F minus competitor means higher rate for F, not a throughput or BLER change. The base order is joint LMMSE, GLSNet, followed **only in A** by DnCNN. *DnCNN baseline prediction* is available for A–D in the waveform diagnostic; this separate receiver-correction control includes DnCNN only on A. Do not count additional arms or estimators on the same slots as new replications.
- Arm order: base, old-D, old-F, new-D, new-F, new-MSE, rotation-only. `alpha[base,arm,3]` and `scale[base,arm]` are the frozen real coefficients and LLR scales. The rotation-only arm uses only the first coefficient.
- `group_size[slot,group]` counts data REs. Group order: all; four gain bands (“deep”, “low”, “moderate”, “strong”) for combined gain ||H||²/2, cut at 0.1, 10^−0.5 and 1 (threshold goes to upper bin); spectral notch; not a spectral notch. A notch is a tone with mean gain over its data symbols less than 0.1 times the equally tone-weighted slot mean. The notch partition and gain partition overlap; do not add their totals together.
- `ce_sum[slot,base,group,method]`: sum over group REs of bit CE, first averaged over six QAM bits **in nats per bit**. Here base order is *only* joint LMMSE and GLSNet; method order is new-F, new-MSE. A group's pooled rate is 6 − (6/ln 2) × summed CE / group RE count. F−MSE equals (6/ln 2) × (summed CE_MSE − summed CE_F) / count. A subgroup's contribution to the overall difference equals its difference times its fraction of all REs, not its fraction of slots.
- `sample_head.npz`: deterministic A/GLSNet example (first physical slot; four specified flattened data-RE indices in OFDM-symbol, then subcarrier order), containing h0, h_p, C_e, complex noise variance, selected coefficients and the corresponding corrected two-receiver vectors for **all seven** arms saved from the research forward pass. These are already-computed pilot-derived features, not raw pilots. Test against [head.py](head.py); the synthetic example is not a substitute for population validation.

## Recomputed decisions and their boundaries

The original 18 prespecified comparisons are F−D and F−initial in all four profiles for LMMSE and GLSNet, plus the new-vs-old (F−D) interaction in A for each. Use 20,000 paired whole-slot bootstrap draws with replacement within fixed profile and 5-dB receive-SNR strata; per-run RNG seed 991301. Each tail probability for a primary comparison is 0.05/18. Confirmation uses a strictly positive lower quantile and positive mean. The upper quantile is reported for numerical reproduction; together these endpoints are **not** simultaneous two-sided 95% intervals. Other head comparisons use nominal pointwise 2.5/97.5 percentiles, with no confirmatory decision. Independently including DnCNN on the same A slots does not add any tests to the primary family.

The code also reports all exploratory new-F minus new-MSE gain bands and notch strata for LMMSE and GLSNet. The interval is the paired, whole-slot ratio-of-sums delta interval, with a Student-t quantile (N−1 degrees of freedom). Zero-membership slots remain part of the population. If fewer than 20 slots contribute, the uncertainty is null; no REs means the rate and difference are also null. These are pointwise summaries, not selection-corrected primary results. The MSE-head absolute rate and signed paired difference in each group permit reconstruction of the F rate with the *same* denominator.

The recorded [reference.json](reference.json) contains original means, decisions, bounds and nulls without paths or training logs. Numerical recomputation uses tolerances rather than promising bit-identical floating-point results across libraries. Expected decisions: 8/8 F−initial, 5/8 F−D, and 1/2 A interactions (14/18 in total). DnCNN-A F−D is positive while its F−MSE is negative; both are secondary. B GLSNet notch support is 3,201 REs from 44 slots. C notch support is zero, and D notch support is only four slots.

## Observational phase-versus-orthogonal check (separate from the head tests)

`python controls/mode_check.py` recomputes the eight standardized mean differences quoted in the letter for LMMSE/GLSNet, using **all** data REs from the same A–D slots, not only the 64 sampled regression REs. A phase- or orthogonal-dominated observation has **more than 60%** of total channel-error energy in that mode. `data/phase_cells.npz` stores per profile/base aggregated cell counts, counts of contributing physical slots and sums of (signed calibrated CE excess in nats/bit, linear combining SNR γ, relative squared error E/P); the mode column order is phase then complex-orthogonal. The 100 cells cross the two 10-bin axes: γ (dB) cut at 0,5,…,40 and E/P (dB) cut at −40,−35,…,0, including underflow and overflow bins. Each cell index is 10 × SNR-bin + error-bin. Only cells with ≥200 REs and ≥20 contributing slots **in both groups** enter; weights are proportional to the smaller RE count. The script also gives the separately standardized mean γ and E/P for each mode to show residual imbalance. The weighted excess-CE difference is positive in all eight cases. This is a descriptive association under coarse-bin matching, not a causal rotation experiment. The frozen cell totals suffice for the point estimates reported here, not to refit a new model or reconstruct arbitrary within-cell uncertainty estimates.
